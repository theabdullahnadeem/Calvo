How we think about it

Most websites are a stack of screenshots. The work is the space between them — the half second where a page decides what it wants to be. Get that wrong and nothing else you did matters.

How it runs

Four stages · 6–11 weeks
01Week 1

Brief

Two days taking apart what you asked for until we find what you meant. You get the gap in writing before anyone opens a design file.

02Week 2–3

Direction

One route, built properly. No mood-board buffet, no three options designed to make the middle one look safe.

03Week 4–9

Build

Design and code happen in the same week, in the same room. Motion is prototyped in the browser, never guessed from a video.

04Week 10–11

Ship

Handoff includes the easing curves, the timings and the reasons. It survives your next developer, which is the only real test.
