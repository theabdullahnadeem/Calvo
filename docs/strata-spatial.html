02 — Thesis

Flat screens taught a generation that software has no weight. We disagree. Every interface we build has a floor, a horizon and a camera — so people stop reading a page and start moving through it.

Founded

2019, in a former print shop in Mile End, Montréal. Nineteen people across three time zones today.

Practice

Spatial interface design, motion systems, real-time 3D and the front-end engineering that ships them.

Method

We prototype in code from week one. If it can't hold frame rate, it isn't a design — it's a mood board.

Clients

Consumer hardware, fintech, sports, streaming and three companies we're not allowed to name yet.

07 — Process

Four weeks
to a moving
prototype

Week 01

Pressure test

We start by arguing with the brief. What is the one thing this product must make somebody feel in the first four seconds? Everything after this is in service of that answer, and anything that isn't gets cut here — while it's still cheap.

Creative directionReference cutSuccess metric
Week 02

Build the rig

Camera, grid, light and easing curves get defined as a system before a single screen is designed. This is the part most studios skip, and it's why their motion falls apart on the third page.

Depth specEasing libraryGrid + type scale
Week 03

Prototype in code

A real build in the browser, on a real device, at real frame rate. You scroll it yourself instead of watching a video of someone else scrolling it. Feedback gets specific fast when the thing is in your hand.

Live prototypePerf budgetInteraction pass
Week 04

Ship & hand over

Production code, documented motion tokens, and a two-hour session with your engineers so the system survives after we leave. We stay on retainer for the first release cycle.

Production buildMotion tokensTeam handover
08 — Studio

Nineteen people
who argue
about easing

STRATA° started in 2019 when three of us left larger agencies with the same complaint: the motion always got value-engineered out of the build. So we made a studio where the designers ship the code.

We take on eight to ten engagements a year. That's deliberate — it's the number where the founders can still be in every project, and where we can say no to work that only wants a skin.
